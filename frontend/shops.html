💡 Farmer Advisory: What to Check Before Purchasing Farm Inputs
1. Insist on a Printed Cash Memo Always demand an official GST bill or cash memo mentioning the product trade name, manufacturer, batch number, and expiry date. This is mandatory for claiming warranty or government crop relief.
2. Check Certified Seed Tags Verify the official National / State Seeds Corporation tag. Certified seeds carry a blue certification tag; foundation seeds carry a white tag. Never purchase loose or unlabelled seeds.
3. CIB&RC Registration for Chemicals Check for the Central Insecticides Board (CIB&RC) registration number and toxicity diamond (green, blue, yellow, or red triangle) on pesticide containers. Do not buy damaged or unsealed bottles.
4. Carry Your Soil Health Card Consult your Soil Health Card recommendations before purchasing chemical fertilizers (Urea, DAP, MOP) to avoid excessive input expenditure and preserve long-term soil fertility.
