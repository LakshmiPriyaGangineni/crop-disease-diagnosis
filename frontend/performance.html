Macro Average vs. Weighted Average Metrics

Understanding the distinction between unweighted macro metrics and sample-weighted metrics.

Macro Average treats every disease class equally regardless of sample volume, highlighting performance on smaller web-sourced classes. Weighted Average weights each class's score by its test sample count, reflecting overall volume performance on high-prevalence agricultural classes.
