Academic & Safety Guidelines

CropCure AI provides computer-vision based preliminary assessments. Machine-learning predictions should never replace in-person physical crop inspection by certified agricultural scientists, especially before applying restricted or chemical treatments.

🔬 Preliminary Screening: Visual diagnosis models classify image patterns but cannot inspect roots, stem vascular bundles, soil moisture, or microscopic spore structures.
🧪 No Auto-Pesticides: CropCure AI strictly avoids prescribing chemical pesticide doses. Only qualified extension officers can calculate safe field dosage rates.
🔒 Privacy Assured: Device location is optional. Crop images and consultation notes stay strictly on your local browser and are never uploaded to third parties without your action.

When to Consult an Agricultural Expert

While AI visual diagnosis provides rapid initial screening, you should contact your local KVK or Agriculture Officer in these situations:

⚠️

1. Uncertain Visual Diagnosis (< 50% Confidence)

When visual symptoms overlap among multiple diseases or confidence is low, laboratory inspection or field examination is essential before taking corrective action.

📈

2. Worsening or Rapidly Spreading Symptoms

If leaf lesions, wilting, or yellowing spreads quickly across your field despite initial sanitation or cultural measures.

🔄

3. Recurring Disease Outbreaks / Soil Issues

If the disease reappears in successive seasons, suggesting soil-borne pathogens, resistant strains, or micronutrient deficiencies.

🧪

4. Chemical Treatment & Fungicide Decisions

Never apply chemical fungicides, insecticides, or restricted formulations without confirming the target pathogen, label dose, waiting periods, and safety intervals.
